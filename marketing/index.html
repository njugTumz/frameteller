From your voice to an animatic

Four of Meta's models each do one job in the pipeline.

  1. Speak

    Narrate the scene. Muse Voice Transcribe writes it down live and tells co-directors apart.

  2. Direct

    Muse Spark breaks your words into shots, framing and dialogue, and keeps one description per character.

  3. Draw

    Muse Image draws each panel from character references, so faces and outfits stay consistent.

  4. Refine and export

    Click anything in a panel and SAM 3.1 selects it to move or change. Export a timed animatic.
