Scene one. Night. A narrow alley in the rain. Mira, a girl in a yellow raincoat with a red scarf, walks home alone.
She hears a tiny sound under a wooden crate. She kneels down and looks.
Close on the crate. A small grey cat is hiding there, and its eyes glow like lanterns.
Mira smiles and holds out her hand. The cat steps into the light.
Wide shot. Mira walks out of the alley with the cat on her shoulder. The rain stops.
Close-up on the cat. It looks back at the alley, and the glow in its eyes fades.
