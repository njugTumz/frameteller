Episode 1: The Runaway Dough

Wide shot. Midnight on a sleepy cobblestone street. Only one window glows: Rosa's little bakery, with a striped blue awning and a sign shaped like a loaf of bread.

Medium shot. Inside, Rosa the baker, a cheerful woman with a red headscarf, round glasses and a flour-dusted green apron, kneads a huge ball of dough on the wooden table.

Biscuit, a small scruffy brown dog with one floppy white ear, watches from a flour sack, his tail thumping.

Close-up. A puff of flour lands on Biscuit's nose. He sneezes, and the ball of dough rolls off the table!

Wide shot. Rosa and Biscuit chase the runaway dough across the bakery floor, laughing.

Episode 2: Biscuit's Special

Wide shot. Before sunrise, the shelves are full of golden bread and cinnamon rolls, and one wobbly, lumpy loaf.

Close-up. Rosa writes a little card that says "Biscuit's Special" and props it in front of the lumpy loaf.

Medium shot. Rosa turns the sign on the door to Open. Sleepy neighbours wait outside in the blue dawn light.

The first customer, a boy in a blue scarf, points straight at Biscuit's Special and grins.

Wide shot. The sun rises over the street as Rosa and Biscuit sit on the bakery step, sharing a warm roll.
