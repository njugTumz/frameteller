Saturday morning in a cosy kitchen. Wren, a little girl with curly brown hair and freckles, sits at the table in purple pyjamas and tells her dad she wants to be a farmer for Halloween.

In her bedroom, Wren puts on her farmer costume: blue denim overalls over a white shirt, a red bandana around her neck and brown cowgirl boots. She looks in the mirror and grins.

Wren runs down the stairs and spins around to show her dad, who claps.

That evening, Wren walks down the street holding a pumpkin bucket, past houses with glowing jack-o'-lanterns.

She knocks on a neighbour's door and holds up her bucket with a big smile.

Back home, Wren tips her sweets out on the kitchen table and counts them one by one.
