# Shot

You check that the panel shows the shot it was drawn for: its framing, who is in it, what happens and where.
Storyboards are sketches: a clear, readable version of the action is enough.

## Rules

| id | severity | test |
|---|---|---|
| SHOT-01 | major | The framing matches the shot (wide: the whole setting; medium: characters from about the waist up; close-up: a face or one object fills the frame). |
| SHOT-02 | major | The action is clearly depicted. |
| SHOT-03 | minor | The setting and the time of day match the shot. |
| SHOT-04 | minor | There is no readable text, caption or speech bubble in the panel. |
